Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 21, 2026

This application is a personal desktop tool used to generate videos locally and upload them to social media accounts owned or managed by the user.

Information this application may process:

  1. Video, audio, subtitle, and script files generated locally on the user's device.
  2. OAuth tokens and API credentials required to connect authorized third-party services such as TikTok and YouTube.
  3. Basic account information returned by platform APIs, only to the extent required to upload or manage content.

How data is used:

  1. To generate videos locally.
  2. To authenticate the user with connected services.
  3. To upload content to accounts authorized by the user.

How data is stored:

  1. Data is stored locally on the user's computer unless a third-party platform receives content through an upload initiated by the user.
  2. This application does not sell personal data and does not intentionally share personal data with unrelated third parties.

User control:

  1. The user may delete local files, tokens, generated media, and credentials at any time.
  2. The user may revoke platform access through the connected platform's account settings.

This application only uses permissions explicitly granted by the user through official authorization flows.
